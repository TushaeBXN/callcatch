# Sales follow-up / lead reactivation — NOT BUILT (placeholder)

This is a planned second product: contacting a client's old leads to win back
business. **Do not build it yet.** It has legal requirements the receptionist
does not.

Before any work starts, it will need:

- **A2P 10DLC registration.** US carriers require business text messaging from
  standard 10-digit numbers ("application-to-person") to be registered, both the
  brand and each messaging campaign. Unregistered traffic can be blocked.
  VERIFY: current registration steps, fees, and review times.
- **Consent records.** Proof that each person agreed to be contacted, and how.
  Outbound calls and texts are governed by the TCPA (Telephone Consumer
  Protection Act) and state laws. VERIFY with an attorney.
- **Opt-out handling.** Honor STOP / "don't contact me" immediately and
  permanently, across every channel.
